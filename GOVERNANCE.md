# Proposed governance

This proposal is open to revision with the first contributors.

Adrian Ripley is the project's initiator. Initially, repository administration
rests with the initiator; no other maintainers have been appointed. Decentralized
protocol goals do not imply that project administration is already distributed.

## Decisions

Discuss ideas publicly before turning them into implementation tasks. Changes to
the protocol, trust model or advertised guarantees need a written proposal with
alternatives, costs and a verification plan.

Seek consensus and record objections. For non-urgent structural decisions, the
proposed comment period is at least one week. If agreement is not reached, the
responsible maintainer records the decision, reasoning and conditions for review.
A decision does not count as experimental validation.

## Responsibilities

Consistent contributions and reliable reviews can lead to a public proposal for
a new maintainer with a defined scope and that person's consent. As collaborators
become available, aim to distribute repository and release administration across
at least two people. Document roles and recovery procedures without exposing secrets.

## Community spaces

Technical criticism and disagreement are welcome. Harassment, threats, spam and
non-consensual disclosure of personal information are not. Moderation decisions
should be proportionate, explained and reviewable by another maintainer when available.
These rules govern collaboration spaces, not authorization of network messages.

## Public commitments

Announcements must reflect demonstrated capabilities. Partnerships, paid work and
security guarantees require an explicit project decision. Any future funding should
have documented management, intended use and relevant conflicts of interest.

Review this governance proposal with the first contributors, particularly the
decision process, appointment of maintainers and resolution of conflicts.
